Being

Hi, I'm Evelyn — a product leader who has navigated more chaos than the job description mentioned.

By day I'm a product leader and operator — someone who has spent nearly a decade building data products, launching features across diverse markets, and navigating some of the most complex environments in tech. From managing analytics teams at Capital One to shaping growth strategies at Uber, to developing AI-powered enterprise solutions at insightsoftware, my career has never been a straight line. It's been a series of pivots, each one teaching me something the previous chapter couldn't.

I've also been laid off. And restructured out of roles I loved. Each time, I had a choice: treat it as a verdict, or treat it as a signal. I chose the signal — and each time, I came out the other side with more clarity than I had going in.

The chaos is inevitable in any career. The clarity is possible — if you know how to look for it.

That's the tension I live in, and the one I think about most. It's what I write about. It's what I speak about. And it's what I bring into every room I'm in — whether that's a product team trying to do more with less, a conference stage, or a 1:1 conversation with someone trying to figure out what's next.

Outside of my day job, I've found real meaning in the Women in Product community — organizing AI implementation workshops, leading panel discussions on data strategy, and building spaces where women leading technical products can think out loud without performing certainty they don't have. Because the best breakthroughs I've seen — in products and in people — happen when someone finally feels safe enough to say I don't know, let's figure it out together.

If you're a mid-career professional who feels stuck — not because you lack ability, but because the path forward isn't visible yet — I think we'd have a lot to talk about.

Co-Creating

You bring the context and the will to move.
